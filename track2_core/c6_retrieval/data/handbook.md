# Nodi Labs Employee Handbook (fictional company, made up for practice)

## 1. Working hours
Our core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Sunday to Thursday. Outside core hours you can plan your own time, as long as you work 40 hours a week. Friday and Saturday are the weekly holidays. If you need to be away during core hours, tell your team lead in the team channel before 10:00 that day. Late starts are fine occasionally, but repeated late starts without notice will be discussed in your monthly check in.

## 2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after completing their first three months. Remote days must be agreed with your team lead one week in advance. During remote days you must be reachable on chat within 30 minutes during core hours. Working from outside Bangladesh needs written approval from HR, because of tax and data protection rules. The office provides a monthly internet allowance of 1,500 taka for employees with regular remote days.

## 3. Leave
Every full time employee gets 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and carried over days expire on 31 March. Sick leave is separate: you have 14 days of paid sick leave per year, and a doctor's note is required for sick leave longer than 2 days in a row. Parental leave is 16 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## 4. Public holidays
The office follows the government public holiday list for Bangladesh. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is not moved to another day. For Eid, the office closes for the official holidays plus one extra day, announced by HR at least two weeks before. If your project needs you to work on a public holiday, you receive one replacement day off, to be used within 60 days.

## 5. Equipment
New employees receive a laptop on their first day. The laptop remains company property and must be returned within 3 working days of your last day. You may request one external monitor and one headset. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. Do not install unlicensed software; IT can provide approved alternatives for most tools.

## 6. Security
Use a password manager for all work accounts. Two step verification is mandatory for email, chat and code hosting. Never share customer data in public channels or personal messaging apps. Suspicious emails should be forwarded to security@nodilabs.example and then deleted. A security training session is held every quarter, and attendance is required.

## 7. Expenses
Work related expenses are reimbursed if they are approved before you spend the money. Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Taxi and ride sharing costs are covered for trips between 21:00 and 06:00, or when carrying company equipment. Meals are covered only during approved business travel, up to 800 taka per day. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## 8. Learning budget
Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 25,000 taka for courses, books and conference tickets. Online course subscriptions are allowed if they relate to your role. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year. You can also take up to 3 paid learning days per year to attend a conference or workshop. After a course, share one short summary with your team so others can learn too.

## 9. Performance reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Before each review, you write a short self assessment and choose two colleagues to give feedback. Salary changes are discussed only in the December review. If you disagree with your review, you can ask for a second meeting with HR present within 14 days.

## 10. Leaving the company
The notice period is one month during probation and two months after probation. Probation lasts three months. On your last day, you return all equipment, and HR holds a short exit interview. Your final salary, including payment for unused annual leave, is paid within 30 days of your last day.
